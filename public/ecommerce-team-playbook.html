Slide 2 of 7 · Inventory & Production

From Raw Parts to Finished Bottles

Just like baking a cake, you can't make a finished bottle unless all the ingredients are in stock. Our system uses a simple 2-Step Stock System.

🍲 The "Recipe" Rule (Bill of Materials)
When you click Run Production Batch to make finished bottles, the system checks if you have enough raw parts in stock:
To make 1 Hair Oil Bottle, you need:
💧 Liquid Base + 🍾 Bottle + 🏷️ Label + 📦 Outer Box
(No pump needed for Hair Oil!)
To make 1 Shampoo Bottle, you need:
💧 Liquid Base + 🍾 Bottle + 🏷️ Label + 🧴 Pump/Cap + 📦 Outer Box

✅ If any part is missing (for example, 0 labels left), the system stops you and tells you what to restock first.

🌍 Two Warehouses: Pakistan vs. Qatar
The system tracks where your bottles physically sit so you never accidentally sell a bottle in Qatar that is sitting in Pakistan:
🇵🇰 Pakistan Stock

Used when you log a sale under:
• Shopify (Pakistan)
• Organic (Pakistan)

🇶🇦 Qatar Stock

Used when you log a sale under:
• Salon (Old Airport / Wakrah)
• Organic (Qatar) & Backbar

✈️ Sending Stock to Qatar? Just click "Transfer to Qatar" in Inventory. It subtracts the bottles from Pakistan and adds them to Qatar in one click!
Slide 3 of 7 · Pricing & Profit Page New Feature: Bundle / Tier Stacker

How to Build High-Profit Bundles

When someone buys only 1 bottle, courier delivery and Facebook ad costs eat most of our profit. Selling a 2 to 4 item bundle gives us much more cash per order!

How to Use the Bundle / Tier Stacker
Go to Pricing & Profit and scroll down to the Bundle / Tier Stacker box before launching any new bundle offer:
  • 1. Pick 2 to 4 Products: Choose the products you want to bundle together. The system adds up their exact manufacturing cost automatically.
  • 2. Click "Use 3x COGS": Our golden rule for paid ads is to price a bundle at least 3 times its cost (which gives a healthy 66.7% profit margin).
  • 3. Check Your Profit in PKR: Make sure the Total Gross Profit is green and high enough to easily pay for shipping and ads!

Slide 5 of 7 · Ecommerce KPIs Page New Feature: Day-0 Cash Check

Are Our Ads Making Money on Day 1?

On the Ecommerce KPIs page, the top card is called Day-0 Net Contribution (New Customers). In plain English, it answers one question: "After paying for the product, courier delivery, and Facebook ads, how much cash is left from each new customer's first order?"

What the Green & Red Flags Mean
Simple Math:
New Customer Sales − (Product Cost + Shipping + Ad Spend) ÷ Number of New Customers
✓ Infinite Scale (Green)

Above PKR 0: Every new customer makes us instant profit on their very first order! We can safely spend more on ads to grow faster.

⚠ Bleeding Cash (Red)

Below PKR 0: Ads or shipping are costing more than the first order makes. Pause bad ads and push higher-priced bundles!

Slide 6 of 7 · Salon Conversion & Marketing

Salon Wash-Basin Sales & Ad Testing

Two more tools the team uses every week: tracking how well Qatar salon stylists recommend products, and testing which ad videos perform best.

💇‍♀️ Qatar Salon: Wash Basin → Reception
In Qatar, our stylists use Laleen shampoo and hair oil on clients at the wash basin. That is our best free advertisement!
  • 1. When a bottle is opened for salon use: Log it in Sales & Usage under channel "Backbar Use (Qatar)" (price is automatically PKR 0).
  • 2. When a client buys a bottle at the front desk: Log it under "Salon (Old Airport)" or "Salon (Wakrah)".
  • 3. Our Goal (100%+ Score): For every 1 bottle used at the wash basin, stylists should sell at least 1 bottle at the reception desk!
📣 Marketing Hub: Content & Ads Board
Under Marketing & Creative, we manage our social posts and paid ads in two simple screens:
  • 1. Content Calendar: Plan each post or video hook, assign who is filming it, and upload the image/video preview.
  • 2. Meta Ads Board (3 Columns):
    • Testing: New ads we are trying out.
    • Scaling: Winning ads that bring cheap leads (Qatar) or high sales (Pakistan)—we click Promote to Scaling!
    • Killed: Ads that didn't work and were turned off.
